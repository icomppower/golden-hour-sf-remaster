# Divergence Log

Every intentional deviation from upstream `icomppower/sf-sunset-drive`
(@ `8fa56535ffde138a17b2885de9ee53f0c054f005`). Mirrored into section 11 of the
Notion spec. No silent divergence.

| Date | What diverged | Why | Back-port? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-09-09 | Renderer replaced with a baked glTF pipeline | The whole point of the remaster | No |
| 2026-09-09 | `cities/san_francisco.js` is consumed by *running* upstream `build()` under real three.js in Node (`build/city_to_json.mjs`), not by re-implementing its data | The upstream RNG stream is consumed in order, so road ribbons, building placement, tree scatter and landmark geometry come out bit-identical. Terrain mesh vs `terrainH` agrees to 2.3e-5 (float32 noise) | No |
| 2026-09-09 | Terrain/bridge/switchback functions, the water shader and the traffic/police/cable-car actors are **generated** into `src/sim/` and `src/render/` as verbatim source slices, not hand-copied | A hand copy drifts; a generated one cannot. Re-running `npm run extract` after an upstream re-copy regenerates them and fails loudly if the upstream shape changed | No |
| 2026-09-09 | `assets/landmarks/*.blend` are authored by `build/make_landmark_assets.py` from the extracted upstream silhouettes (bevel, smooth-by-angle, real PBR, joined into one Tier A object) rather than modelled by hand | No Blender GUI in this environment, and it removes the section-12 long pole outright: silhouettes stay exactly on the upstream coordinates while the materials and shading are remastered. The files are real `.blend`s, so a hand-authored replacement can be dropped in per type | No |
| 2026-09-09 | The engine's realtime sun / hemi / ambient lights are stripped at runtime by the city module (`stripRuntimeLights`) | Keeps `src/sim/engine.js` byte-identical to upstream while baked lighting owns the scene | No |
| 2026-09-09 | Environment is a Blender sky render (multiple-scattering / Nishita) exported as a 512×256 equirect KTX2 and PMREM'd once at load, instead of a Poly Haven HDRI pre-filtered offline | No HDRI download in the pipeline, and there is no offline encoder here for three's PMREM cube-UV layout. Cost is a one-off prefilter at load, not per frame, so the perf gate is unaffected. Spec §5 asked for offline pre-filtering | No |
| 2026-09-09 | Cycles bakes run on **CPU** by default (`--device gpu` to retry) | Blender 5.2.1's Metal backend crashes in `MetalKernelPipeline::compile` (binary-archive serialisation) partway through a multi-tier bake on this machine. CPU bakes the whole city in ~3.5 min, so the pipeline is not GPU-bound | No |
| 2026-09-09 | Water stays the upstream runtime shader and is excluded from the bake | It is animated; a baked lightmap would freeze the sun glitter. Spec §1 keeps materials in scope but the water look is upstream's and unchanged | No |
| 2026-09-09 | Chunk grid is 4×4 = 16 chunks **plus** a `core` chunk | Objects larger than a chunk (the two bridges) would pop as a unit; core stays resident | No |
| 2026-09-09 | The irradiance volume is baked by turning every probe into one up-facing quad UV-mapped to a single texel of a 256×192 image, so one Cycles pass fills all 49 152 probes | A per-probe render loop would take hours; this is a genuine Cycles bake that finishes in seconds. Probes keep camera visibility on — clearing every ray-visibility flag drops them out of the BVH and the bake returns black | No |
| 2026-09-09 | Lit windows are an emission texture on the shared geometry whose strength the runtime crossfades per set, rather than separate night materials | Geometry (and therefore the GLB) stays shared between lighting sets, which is what makes the night swap a texture rebind | No |
| 2026-09-09 | The engine's sun is kept for dynamic objects only: baked materials zero `reflectedLight.direct*`, shadow maps are off, and soft contact discs anchor the cars instead | three has no per-object light filtering, so a realtime sun would double-light the baked city. Cars are not baked and would otherwise float, unlit, on a lightmapped street | No |
| 2026-09-09 | Lightmap payload is applied with gain 1.0, not the textbook pi | Measured, not assumed: Cycles' colourless Diffuse Direct+Indirect bake already lands in three's `irradiance` units. Baking a pi in blew the terrain to pure white | No |
| 2026-09-09 | The four landmark viewpoints of the visual gate are framed from the placed geometry (bounding box + sun axis) rather than hand-placed | Hand-placed cameras pointed at hillsides — the rubric then scores the terrain instead of the landmark | No |
| 2026-09-09 | Landmark identity is tagged by rewriting the upstream landmark loop in a throwaway copy of the module, not inferred from proximity | Proximity (greedy, then two exact DP formulations) kept mis-splitting adjacent landmarks — Fisherman's Wharf against the Lombard marker, the stadium ring against the Coca-Cola bottle — and every authored asset inherits that mistake. The rewrite asserts the upstream line first, so a shape change fails the build | No |
| 2026-09-09 | The Lombard switchback is **carved into the baked terrain** (corridor locally subdivided, then displaced to the road height) | Upstream builds the terrain mesh from `terrainH` but drives the car on `groundH`, and `groundH` drops 57 m into Russian Hill along the switchback. Lombard Street — Tier A hero geometry in this spec — was therefore buried inside the hill and the car drove through rock. No coordinate moves: the ground is remastered into the surface the physics already uses, and only the switchback is carved (`groundH` also raises ground to bridge-deck height, which would wall in the bridges). The Python port is asserted against the extractor's oracle every build | **Yes** — upstream renders the same buried street |
| 2026-09-09 | Touch controls split into a driving row and a compact auxiliary row, with sizes that fit a 390 px phone, plus a NIGHT button | Upstream lays eight controls in one flex row: on a 390 px phone the gas button renders at x=564, off-screen and untappable, so **the car cannot be driven at all**. Gate 1b now hit-tests every control with `elementFromPoint` at 390x844 and asserts the throttle actually accelerates | **Yes** — every city in the Golden Hour series has this |
